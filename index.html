Fine-Grained Car Make Classification

Goal Tell 75 car makes apart from a single photo, where the classes differ by a badge and a bumper line.

Result 87.55% top-1 on CompCars, 4.65 points above the published OverFeat baseline.

Five CNN architectures compared on 30,955 images across 75 makes and 431 models, from a CNN trained from scratch through ResNet50, EfficientNet-B0, and a Squeeze-and-Excitation variant, up to a hierarchical multi-task model with a shared backbone and dual heads. The auxiliary model-recognition task acts as a regularizer on the make head. EfficientNet-B0 reaches 844 images per second against ResNet50's 411, at a sixth of the parameters.

PyTorch, torchvision, CUDA, transfer learning, attention, multi-task learning

Weather Classification with Engineered Features and SVM

Goal Classify weather from a photo without deep learning, then find out how far the feature design travels.

Result 96.04% on MWD, and the same descriptor holds at 90.6% on a differently distributed dataset.

The pipeline is fully classical: hand-engineered colour and Difference-of-Gaussian descriptors, 1562 dimensions reduced to 200 by PCA, and a GridSearch-tuned SVM. In the cross-dataset study the same feature design, carried to a differently distributed dataset with the classifier refit, holds up at 90.6%. That number says how far a colour-specialised descriptor carries outside the dataset it was designed on. The fitted models ship, so the classifier runs without downloading anything.

scikit-learn, OpenCV, PCA, SVM, feature engineering

Deep Reinforcement Learning for Snake

Goal Learn Snake from sparse rewards, and measure how much observability and reward design change the result.

Result 196% over the heuristic baseline, and the agent that sees less plays better than the one that sees everything.

PPO agents on a custom Gymnasium environment, compared under full and partial observability and across three reward designs. The agent restricted to a 5x5 window around its own head outperformed the agent that could see the whole board, and it is the only one that ever completes a board. The step-penalty variant shows reward hacking: integrated over the horizon, the penalty exceeded two fruit rewards, so dying immediately became optimal, and the agent learned to do it.

PyTorch, Stable-Baselines3, Gymnasium, PPO, reward shaping, CUDA

Sunflower: a Two-Axis Solar Tracker

Goal Point a solar array at the brightest light in the room, using a sensor with no fixed reference to regulate against.

Result A working two-axis tracker, built from the gear teeth to the control law, from parts already in storage.

A solar-tracking robot made almost entirely from parts already in storage. Printed body and spur gearbox designed in Fusion and OpenSCAD, custom electronics around an Arduino Nano and an L298N H-bridge, and a closed-loop controller that climbs the gradient of measured luminosity, because the sensor has no fixed setpoint to regulate against and a PID has nothing to hold. The first gearbox stalled the motors and the analogue front end failed under soldering. The report covers both failures and the design changes each one forced.

Arduino, C++, control theory, Autodesk Fusion, OpenSCAD, FDM, sensor calibration
